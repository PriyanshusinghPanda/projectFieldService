# Product

## What it is
Job OS is a **starting point** for a field-service business app: heating, plumbing, electrical, appliance repair, cleaning, lawn and pest, and similar trades.

**One job record runs from the first call to the payment:**
1. a lead;
2. a quote with options, approved by link;
3. a scheduled visit;
4. the technician on site (checklist and signature);
5. the invoice and payment.

Plans add the next visit. It is deliberately small: a business starts here and builds its own way of working on top with Emergent.

## Users
- **Owners and office staff** at small trade businesses (1–20 technicians). They run the day from a calendar, texts, a spreadsheet and an invoicing app that knows nothing about the job.
- **Technicians** on a phone in a van, often with poor signal.
- **The builder:** the owner, an agency or a founder who uses Emergent's agent to shape the template to one business (or to resell it).

## What it must prove in a demo
1. The core journey works end to end with real numbers, in under 5 minutes.
2. It already looks like their business: the trade's words, their brand colour and logo letters, believable demo data.
3. What it doesn't do yet is one prompt away. The "Build next" page lists what owners in this trade ask for most, each with a ready prompt for Emergent's agent.

## Evidence on hand
- **Our builders:** 73 paying field-service builders on Emergent and what they built ($493k paid). See `docs/research/internal-findings.md`.
- **Owners' wants:** a fast external pass on what owners ask for. See `docs/research/external-wants.md` (provisional).
- **Integrations:** which providers power those wants. See `docs/research/integrations-field-service.md`.

## Principles
1. **The job is the hero.** Every screen shows a real record with its customer, money, status and next step.
2. **Small core, clear seams.** One backend file and one React app, so an agent can read the whole thing and extend it safely.
3. **Numbers you can trust.** Money is in integer cents, approved quotes are locked, and no double booking.
4. **Built for the van as much as the office.** Big touch targets, and clear reasons when something blocks the next step.
5. **Specific over generic.** Use the trade's own words, never marketing filler.

## Brand
Calm, exact, dependable. The business's own colour is the one accent, on neutral greys. Text uses the Geist and Geist Mono fonts.
