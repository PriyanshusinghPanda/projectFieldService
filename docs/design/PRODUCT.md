# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Owners and office staff at small and mid-size trade businesses: heating, plumbing, electrical, appliance repair, cleaning, lawn and pest. Right now they run the day from a whiteboard, a shared calendar, a stack of texts and an invoicing app that knows nothing about the job. Also their technicians. A technician opens the app on a phone in a van, in a basement, with gloves on and often with no signal. Secondary audience: the builder or agency who buys the template and reshapes it for one business. They need to see in a few seconds that it is a finished, working product, not a starter kit.

## Product Purpose

Job OS is a field-service app where one job record runs from the first call to the next visit: lead → quote → schedule → technician on site → invoice and payment → review → next visit. The inbox turns a missed call into a lead and a quote. Each quote offers good, better and best options priced from the price book, with tax, margin, deposit and payment stages worked out live. The customer approves and pays the deposit from a link. On the dispatch board you drag a job onto a technician. "Suggest best slot" explains its choice, and a double booking is blocked. The technician app works offline. It won't let a job finish until the checklist, before/after photos and signature are done. Then it goes straight to an invoice and Tap to Pay. Maintenance plans book their own visits. "Make it yours" settings change deposits, commissions and who sees prices with no code. Custom fields show up on the tech app and the invoice, and hooks hold the buyer's own logic. It is built from what 73 paying field-service builders made on Emergent. Success: a trade owner watches one job go from missed call to paid invoice and says "that's my day, done properly."

## Brand Personality

Calm, exact, dependable. Like a well-run dispatch office: nobody shouts, everything has a number and a time, and the next step is always obvious. Confident because the work is complete, not because the copy is loud. Three words: calm, exact, dependable.

## Anti-references

Not a generic SaaS dashboard with a hero, three feature cards and a testimonial grid. No gradient meshes, glowing blobs or "AI" visuals. Not a cluttered legacy field-service ERP with twelve toolbars and grey forms. Not cartoon tradesperson illustrations or hard-hat clip art. No "streamline your workflow", "elevate", "supercharge" or "unlock".

## Design Principles

1. The job is the hero: every screen shows the real job record, with its customer, site, money, status and the next step, rendered as finished UI with realistic demo data.
2. Restraint as the brand: one accent colour taken from the business's own brand, neutrals tinted to it, hairlines instead of boxes, and one featured element per screen (the recommended option, the blocking proof-of-work step, the item that needs you).
3. Numbers you can trust: money in tabular figures, times and job numbers in a mono voice, totals that update live, and statuses with a fixed colour meaning.
4. Built for the van as much as the office: big touch targets, readable in sunlight, clear offline and queued states, and gates that explain what is missing.
5. Specific over generic: use the trade's own words (visit, quote, deposit, proof of work, plan, filter size), never marketing filler.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA contrast minimums across office and technician screens. Keyboard navigable with visible focus. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Status is never conveyed by colour alone; it always has a label too. Touch targets on the technician app are at least 44px.
