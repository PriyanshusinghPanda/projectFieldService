# Demo runbook: one job's journey (about 5 minutes)

**Setup:** `./scripts/dev.sh`, sign in with the **Owner** demo button. Keep the technician app (`/tech`, Technician button) in a second window sized like a phone.

| # | Beat | Click | The line that lands |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Missed call at 9:14 PM | Inbox → Tom Reyes (missed call, texted back) | "Nobody calls back? It already texted them." |
| 2 | The quote | Quotes → Chloe Cho "AC repair or replace": options, tax, deposit → Copy customer link | "Options from your price book, priced in seconds." |
| 3 | The customer approves | Open the `/q/…` link → pick the new system → sign → pay deposit | "Approved and deposit paid from her phone. No app, no login." |
| 4 | Schedule it | Schedule → the new job in Unscheduled → book Marco at 1 PM; try to double-book → blocked | "The right tech, no clashes." |
| 5 | On site | Technician window → the job → On my way → Arrived → Start → Finish (blocked) → checklist + signature → Finish | "No proof of work, no finished job." |
| 6 | Paid | Invoices → the new invoice → Record payment (or the `/pay/…` link) | "Paid before the van leaves." |
| 7 | Next visit | Plans → Sell a plan to Chloe → the first visit appears in Unscheduled | "The next visit books itself." |
| 8 | Make it yours | Settings (brand colour, tax, deposit) → **Build next** page: pick a feature, copy its prompt | "Anything else your business needs is one prompt away on Emergent." |

**"Can it do X?"**
- **Already in the template:** say yes and show it.
- **Not in the template:** open **Build next**, find the card and show its prompt.
